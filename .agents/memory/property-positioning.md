---
name: Property positioning and existing customers
description: Scope of the property-professional messaging pivot relative to existing customer data.
---

Make new-user messaging and demo examples property-first, but do not retroactively change existing businesses' services, customers, or bookings just to align their data with marketing copy.

**Why:** The property pivot was requested as a positioning and demo-data change, with booking infrastructure and existing data preserved. Replacing actual business records would be a separate, destructive migration.

**How to apply:** Use property examples for new-user entry points and seeded demos. Keep legacy customer data intact; get explicit approval before a data migration or removal of existing customer options.
