---
name: Property positioning and existing customers
description: Scope of the property-professional messaging pivot relative to existing customer data.
---

Present the product as a clear three-part journey: property businesses discover BookFlow on the landing page and use the BookFlowX iOS app to manage viewings; buyers and tenants use the Visual Canvas customer booking experience through the shared link or QR code, with no customer app download required. Keep new-user messaging and demo examples property-first, but do not retroactively change existing businesses' services, customers, or bookings to match marketing copy.

**Why:** The user clarified that BookFlow is the property-business landing page and booking system, BookFlowX is the business's iOS management app, and Visual Canvas is the customer-facing booking experience. The property pivot is a positioning change; replacing actual business records would be a separate, destructive migration.

**How to apply:** Keep these product roles distinct in marketing, onboarding, and booking-link explanations. Use property examples for new-user entry points and seeded demos. Keep legacy customer data intact; get explicit approval before a data migration or removal of existing customer options.
