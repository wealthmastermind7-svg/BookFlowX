Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 10, 2026

At BookFlow, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our booking platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, phone number, and any other information you choose to provide when making a booking or setting up a business profile.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to facilitate bookings, communicate with you about your appointments, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption.

4. Data Deletion Requests

Users of BookFlowX may request the deletion of their personal data by contacting our support team at admin@cerolauto.com. To process your request, please include the Business Name or Phone Number associated with your profile.

Steps to Request Deletion:

  • Send an email to admin@cerolauto.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
  • Provide the business name or phone number used in the app.
  • Our team will verify the request and process the deletion within 7 business days.

Data Handling:

When a deletion request is processed, we permanently delete the following types of data:

  • Business profile information (name, description, contact details).
  • Service listings and pricing.
  • Customer contact information.
  • Past and future booking records.

Note on Data Retention: We do not retain any personal data after a deletion request is fulfilled. However, anonymized transaction records related to payments (processed via Stripe) may be kept for a period of up to 5 years as required by financial regulations and tax laws. These records do not contain personal identification details once the account is deleted.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at admin@cerolauto.com.
