Yes — Apple does allow a single app on the App Store to offer both subscription options and a one-time (lifetime) purchase, as long as you implement them using Apple’s official in-app purchase (IAP) mechanisms and comply with the App Store Review Guidelines.

Key points to know:

1. Apple supports multiple types of In-App Purchases in the same app.
You can configure auto-renewable subscriptions, non-consumable products (one-time lifetime purchases), consumables, and non-renewing subscriptions all in one app. Apple’s documentation confirms that apps may offer more than one type of IAP concurrently.  ￼
	•	Auto-renewable subscriptions are recurring (e.g., monthly/annual access to premium content or features).  ￼
	•	Non-consumable products are one-time purchases that do not expire (e.g., “Lifetime Premium Unlock”).  ￼

2. Lifetime access is treated as a non-consumable IAP.
A true “lifetime” deal in App Store terms is just a non-consumable in-app purchase that unlocks features permanently. You set it up separately from subscriptions in App Store Connect.  ￼

3. Many apps already do this.
Developers routinely combine subscription tiers and a one-time lifetime unlock in their paywalls (e.g., some popular productivity and utility apps). This has been discussed in community forums and implementation examples.  ￼

4. Implementation and paywall design.
You must configure each product in App Store Connect and display them correctly in your in-app paywall. Users should clearly see the difference between subscription offers and a lifetime purchase option.  ￼

5. App Review compliance.
The App Review Guidelines do not prohibit mixing subscription and non-consumable purchases. However:
	•	All digital content/features unlocked must be delivered within the app via Apple’s IAP system.
	•	You cannot steer users to external payments for digital content (subject to specific EU exceptions with external links, but those still come with conditions).  ￼

Summary

Monetization Option	Supported Together?	Implementation
Auto-renewable Subscription	Yes	In-App Purchase (Subscription group)
Non-consumable (Lifetime)	Yes	In-App Purchase (Non-consumable)
Consumable	Yes	In-App Purchase (Consumable)
External payments	With restrictions	Must follow Apple’s rules

In short, you can offer both ongoing subscriptions and a one-off lifetime purchase in one app, and Apple’s policy and developer tools support this combination so long as you use Apple’s in-app purchasing correctly throughout the user experience.  ￼
