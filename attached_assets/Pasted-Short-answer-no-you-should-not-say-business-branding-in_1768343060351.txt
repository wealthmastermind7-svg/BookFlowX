Short answer: no, you should not say “business branding” in its current form. That wording is too strong for what the app actually delivers and can trigger both App Store review friction and user trust issues.

Let me be very precise.

⸻

Why “business branding” is risky here

When Apple reviewers and users read “business branding”, they reasonably infer:
	•	Logo upload or replacement
	•	Color or theme customization
	•	Visual brand control (fonts, buttons, accents)
	•	White-label or brand identity features

From what you described, the app currently supports:
	•	Business name display
	•	Possibly service names
	•	Default UI styling (no logo upload, no color control)

That is not branding, it is business identification.

Using “branding” would be considered:
	•	Overstated marketing
	•	Potentially misleading under Apple guideline 2.3.1 (accurate metadata and feature representation)

Apple may not reject immediately, but it increases risk unnecessarily.

⸻

What you should say instead (recommended)

Use language that is:
	•	Accurate
	•	Conservative
	•	Apple-safe
	•	Still value-oriented

Best replacements (ranked)

1. “Business identity” ✅ safest

• Business name shown to customers
• Professional booking page with your business name

2. “Professional business profile” ✅ very good

• Customer-facing booking page with your business details

3. “Customer-facing business page” ✅ neutral

• Branded booking page with your business name

4. “Professional presentation” ⚠️ acceptable but vague
Avoid if possible, but still safer than “branding”.
